# Rule and review methodology

The reference policy model supports five deterministic requirement types:

- required clause: the named clause type must exist;
- prohibited phrase: matching language creates a finding;
- required term: a structured term must be present;
- numeric limit: a value and unit must satisfy an explicit operator;
- allowed value: a structured value must belong to an approved set.

Critical findings and policy conflicts block. High or medium findings require review. Low findings produce a non-compliant result. Scores are weighted indicators for prioritization, not mathematical legal truth.

Every finding cites the policy set, version, rule and affected clause. A missing clause uses a null clause reference because no fabricated clause identity is allowed. Remediation text comes from the approved policy rule and remains a draft until a qualified reviewer decides.

Production thresholds, severity and precedence must be owned by counsel and compliance leaders for each jurisdiction and contract type.
