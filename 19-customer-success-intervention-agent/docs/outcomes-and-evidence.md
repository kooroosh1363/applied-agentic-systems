# Outcomes and evidence claims

The demo can record `no_response`, `meeting_booked`, `usage_improved`, `ticket_resolved`, `renewed`, or `churned`. Recording an event after an intervention does not prove the intervention caused it. Every outcome therefore carries `causalClaimAllowed: false` and `observational_only_without_randomized_control`.

Credible incremental-impact measurement requires a pre-registered experiment, stable assignment, eligible control group, verified exposure, deduplicated outcome events, defined observation window, sample-size planning, guardrail metrics, and analysis of interference and survivorship bias. Project 10 demonstrates controlled experimentation and attribution mechanics; Project 19 does not duplicate or silently bypass those controls.

All included customers, signals, health scores, contacts, and outcomes are synthetic. Green tests prove deterministic software behavior, not reduced churn, improved retention, increased expansion revenue, fairness, legal compliance, or production readiness.
