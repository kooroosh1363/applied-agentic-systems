# Evidence Policy

## Evidence labels

- **Automated:** verified by a deterministic test in CI.
- **Local integration:** verified against the repository's local container stack.
- **Simulated:** produced from synthetic fixtures or mock providers.
- **External sandbox:** verified against a third-party test environment.
- **Production:** requires dated evidence from an authorized production deployment.

Never convert simulated KPIs into customer claims. A statement such as “35% of leads recovered” is permitted only as a clearly labeled scenario unless a real, authorized dataset supports it.
