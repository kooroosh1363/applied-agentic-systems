# KPI and cost

Measure quote cycle time, approval latency, quote acceptance, discount rate, invoice time, days sales outstanding, reconciliation match rate, exception age, partial-payment rate, verified cash collected, and DLQ rate. Cash is counted only from provider-verified reconciled events. Local API cost is zero; hosted cost is `(hosting + messaging + payment fees + accounting fees + finance operations) / paid invoices`. Included business outcomes are simulated.
