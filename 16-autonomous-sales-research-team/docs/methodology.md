# Research methodology

1. Define a lawful research purpose and an explicit domain allowlist.
2. Allocate a bounded sequence of specialized agent steps.
3. Read only approved public-source types through non-destructive tools.
4. Attach at least one citation to every claim.
5. Require two independent publishers for high-importance claims.
6. Reduce confidence for stale, weak, or single-source evidence.
7. Withhold unqualified claims and surface contradictions.
8. Produce a brief with source manifest, trace, and fingerprint.
9. Require an authorized human reviewer and rationale.

The quality score is a transparent heuristic, not statistical truth. Source-type priors are multiplied by independence and freshness factors. Teams must calibrate thresholds against their own reviewed dataset before production use.
